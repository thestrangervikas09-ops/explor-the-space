Mysteries of the universe

BLACK HOLES

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity becomes so strong that nothing that crosses its event horizon can escape — not even light.

Nearest known

Gaia BH1

About 1,560 light-years from Earth, in the direction of the constellation Ophiuchus.

Mass

~10 Solar Masses

Gaia BH1 is a stellar-mass black hole with a mass roughly ten times that of our Sun.

Important idea

Not a cosmic vacuum

A black hole does not simply suck everything around it. Objects can orbit it just as planets orbit stars.

01 — How big?

How big is Gaia BH1?

The mass of a black hole is not the same thing as the diameter of its event horizon. For a non-spinning black hole of around 10 solar masses, the Schwarzschild radius is roughly 30 km, giving an event-horizon diameter of roughly 60 km. This is an approximation based on its estimated mass.

Estimated mass≈ 10 × the mass of the Sun
Approx. event-horizon diameter≈ 60 km for a ~10-solar-mass non-spinning black hole
02 — What's around it?

Which gases are involved?

The black hole itself is not a ball of gas. Matter around a black hole can form an accretion disk, and that material may contain hydrogen, helium and heavier elements. When the gas becomes extremely hot and ionized, it can produce strong radiation, including X-rays.

HydrogenThe most abundant element in normal cosmic gas.
HeliumAnother major component of stellar and interstellar gas.
Ionized plasmaHot charged particles can surround an actively feeding black hole.
X-raysHot accreting material can emit high-energy radiation detectable by telescopes.
Important: We should say “gas around a black hole,” not “gases inside a black hole.” Once matter crosses the event horizon, we cannot directly observe what happens inside.
03 — Beyond BH1

There are other nearby black holes

ESA reported Gaia BH1 at about 1,560 light-years, Gaia BH2 at about 3,800 light-years, and Gaia BH3 at about 1,926 light-years. BH3 is notable for its roughly 33-solar-mass stellar origin and is the heaviest stellar-origin black hole discovered in our galaxy at the time of ESA's announcement.
