Mysteries of the Universe

BLACK HOLES

A black hole is a region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape once it passes the event horizon. The black hole itself is dark, but nearby hot gas and dust can glow in an accretion disk.

Nearest knownGaia BH1≈ 1,560 light-years away
Mass≈ 9.6 × SunStellar-mass black hole
Gas around itHydrogen & heliumHot ionized plasma can emit X-rays
